### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a cybersecurity and security operations enthusiast dedicated to **threat detection, log telemetry analysis, and incident response**. My core focus lies in deploying cloud & on-premises SIEM architectures (**Microsoft Sentinel, Splunk**), authoring detection rules mapped to **MITRE ATT&CK**, analyzing adversarial techniques, and automating triage workflows.

* 🔭 **Currently Working On:** Detection Engineering with KQL & automated threat intelligence enrichment via SOAR.
* 🎯 **Target Roles:** SOC Analyst (Tier 1 / Tier 2), Detection Engineer, Cybersecurity Analyst.
* 💡 **Core Strengths:** Log Analysis (Windows Events & Sysmon), KQL, SPL, Incident Triage, MITRE ATT&CK, Network Traffic Analysis.
* 🎓 **Education:** Rajeev Gandhi Memorial College of Engineering and Technology (RGMCET).
